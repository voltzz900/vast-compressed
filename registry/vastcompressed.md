## Description

A content mod: data only, nothing to build.

- **828 compressed blocks:** 92 of the game's blocks (stones, deepslate, netherrack, end stone, obsidian, terracotta and the 16 concretes, dirt, sand, gravel, clay, mud, snow, every log and plank, moss, hay, wart blocks, pumpkin, melon, coal, iron, gold, copper, raw ores, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald, netherite, glass, glowstone, magma, blue ice and more), each in nine tiers.
- **Crafting:** nine make the next tier in the 3 × 3 grid; one gives nine of the tier below.
- **Each tier is marked** by a frame drawn over the block: steel for I–III, brass for IV–VI, violet for VII–IX. The tooltip says how many blocks it holds.
- Each tier is a quarter harder to mine and to blow up. Glowstone and magma still glow; sand and gravel don't fall.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese. Tags `vastcompressed:compressed` and `vastcompressed:compressed/<block>`.

The overlays are hand-drawn pixel art made in the repository; a block shows the game's own texture by name, none is shipped. Inspired by AllTheCompressed by the All The Mods team; not affiliated with or endorsed by All The Mods.
